# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and evidence-minded community.
Participants must engage without harassment, personal attacks, intimidation,
or discrimination. Disagreement about methods should address evidence,
assumptions, and reasoning rather than the character or motives of contributors.

Project maintainers may clarify expectations, edit or remove inappropriate
contributions, and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
behavior threatens a safe and productive community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the package maintainer listed in
`DESCRIPTION`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially
as circumstances permit.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
